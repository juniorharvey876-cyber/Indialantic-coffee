What We Serve

Something for Everyone

From our famous cold brew to chai that people drive across the state for — plus fresh baked goods and real food to start your day right.

Fan Favorite
Cold Brew

Our most-raved drink. Strong, smooth, and refreshing — customers say it's the best cold brew they've ever had. Try it straight or with gingerbread foam.

Must Try
Dirty Chai

Locals say it's the best chai in all of Florida. Spiced, bold, and perfectly balanced — available hot or iced with your choice of milk.

Specialty
Iced White Mocha

Better than the big chains — and easier on your wallet. Rich, creamy, and made with care every single time.

Something Different
Spritzers & Lotus

Red Bull spritzers and Lotus energy drinks — two unique options you won't find at your average coffee shop. Refreshing and totally worth it.

Fresh Baked
Pastries & Treats

Fresh baked goods from Lovebugs including cronuts, brownies, cookies, and seasonal specialties. Gluten-free and dairy-free options always available.

Breakfast
Bagel Sandwiches

Warm, fresh breakfast sandwiches to fuel your morning. Simple, satisfying, and made right here in our little shop on 5th Ave.
